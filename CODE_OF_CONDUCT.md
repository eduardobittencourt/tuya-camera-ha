# Community guidelines

Everyone is welcome to ask questions, report problems and contribute, regardless
of experience, background or identity. English and Portuguese are both welcome.

Be respectful and specific. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
Give people time to respond, describe disagreements calmly and accept that a
volunteer project cannot promise deadlines or support every device.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual content directed at others,
deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information
are not acceptable. Never post account credentials or private camera images.

The repository maintainer, @eduardobittencourt, moderates issues, discussions and
pull requests. They may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads
or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Block or report user** tools for abuse,
especially when a public reply would reveal personal details. Credential exposure
and software vulnerabilities should use the [security reporting channel](SECURITY.md).
